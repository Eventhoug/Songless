      How to play

      1. Press play and listen to the clip. It starts at 0.5 seconds.
      2. Search for the song and pick it from the list to guess.
      3. A wrong guess or Skip unlocks a longer clip: 0.5, 1, 2, 4, 8 and 16 seconds.
      4. You have 6 tries. A wrong guess by the right artist turns yellow.
      5. Start of song plays from the beginning, Main hook from the chorus or riff.

      Points: 6 for the first try, 5 for the second, and so on. The songs never end.
